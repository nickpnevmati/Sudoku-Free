# Privacy Policy — Sudoku - Free

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

Sudoku - Free ("the app") is developed by NuclearBiscuitGames.

## Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.
There are no accounts, no advertising, and no in-app purchases.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so you can continue where you left off:

- Your current puzzle and your progress in it
- Your settings (theme, error checking, Quicknote, win animation)

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

## Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
